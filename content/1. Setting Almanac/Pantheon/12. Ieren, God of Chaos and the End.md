**Alignment**: Chaotic Evil
**Domain**: The End, Entropy, Chaos
**Common Epithets:** The Last Sigh, The Patient One 
**Public Perception:** Chaotic Evil God of Destruction and Nihilism

## Theology
To the common man, Ieren is a figure of enigmatic terror, synonymous with complete annihilation. He is not a god of noble death or respectful endings; he is the divine malice that ensures all things built will crumble to dust. ieren is eternally patient, not because he is dispassionate, but because he knows that all things, eventually, will break. He has no need to rush, for every moment of decay, every crumbling stone, and every fading hope is a prayer to his long and terrible glory.

## Worshippers and Servants

His servants are few and far between, finding refuge in old and forgotten ruins. 

## Symbols and Manifestations

**Symbols:**

- A crumbling hourglass from which the last grains of sand have fallen.
    
- A single, cold ember in a vast field of grey ash.
    
- A cracked and shattered circle.
    
- The silhouette of a dead, leafless tree.
    

**Manifestations:** Ieren's avatars are said to be subtle harbingers of doom, their appearances always preceding tragedy. He is the chilling omen, the quiet observer of ruin. He might appear as:

- A quiet old man seen watching a town from a nearby hill just before a plague strikes.
    
- A lone, silent traveler on a road where a caravan vanishes without a trace.
    
- A cold, dispassionate voice on the wind that whispers of failures to come in a place of great silence and age.
    

To feel his presence is to feel the sudden, unnerving certainty that something precious is about to end forever.
