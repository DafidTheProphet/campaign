True Neutral

This nearly forgotten God enjoys little worship from the inhabitants of Jetztara. Only a few remote shrines house his relics and worshippers. A relic of the old continent, the spirit of the sun is viewed not as a god, but a force of nature. It's day of remembrance is the twenty-second of his month.
