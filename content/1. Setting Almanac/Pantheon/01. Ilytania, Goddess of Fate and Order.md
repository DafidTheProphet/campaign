**Alignment:** Lawful Good 
**Domains:** Fate, Order, Prophecy, Law, Protection 
**Common Epithets:** The Great Architect

## Theology

Ilytania, Goddess of Fate, Order, and Prophecy, oversees the destiny of the world and those within it. She maintains the edict of **Undeniable Destiny**, a force that cements sequences of events in the stream of time. 

## Morality

That which is orderly is godly. That which is chaotic is anathema. Ilytania and her followers hold order in the highest esteem. 

## The Augurs and Tsugeh Clan
Among the other Gods and Goddesses, Ilytania is one of the most generous with blessings. Those she blesses are known as the **Augurs**, individuals with the gift of prophecy. Each Augur has a unique manifestation of their gift. For example, some receive their visions during certain hours of the day, see prophecies in reflective surfaces, or fall into a prophetic trance while being burnt. Some may see the visions in the same manner, but the potency of their ability is different.

Most Augurs are forcibly recruited by the Tsugeh clan as soon as their ability manifests. To the Tsugeh, being an Augur is the highest calling, a sacred duty that often requires one to live a life apart from the world to better interpret the will of their goddess.

## Symbols and Manifestations

**Symbols:**

- A perfectly woven, unending tapestry.
    
- An unbroken golden circle or an ouroboros biting its own tail.
    
- A single, flawless crystal lens or a calm, all-seeing eye.
    
- A perfectly balanced scale.

**Manifestations**

Ilytania rarely manifests in human form, instead appearing as golden wisps, guiding mortals to their destined path.
