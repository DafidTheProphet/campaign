**Alignment:** Lawful Good 
**Domains:** Fate, Order, Prophecy, Law, Protection 
**Common Epithets:** The Great Architect

## Theology

Ilytania, Goddess of Fate, Order, and Prophecy, oversees the destiny of the world and those within it. She maintains the edict of **Undeniable Destiny**, a force that cements sequences of events in the stream of time. 

## Morality

That which is orderly is godly. That which is chaotic is anathema. Ilytania and her followers hold order in the highest esteem. 

## The Augurs and Tsugeh Clan
Among the other Gods and Goddesses, Ilytania is one of the most generous with blessings. Those she blesses are known as  **augurs**, individuals with the gift of prophecy. Each Augur has a unique circumstance that triggers their gift. For example, some receive their visions during certain hours of the day, see prophecies in reflective surfaces, or fall into a prophetic trance while being burnt. No two individuals possess the same trigger at the same time, though the same trigger can be repeated throughout history. The category of vision that an augur can experience and its trigger are determined at birth. 

The Tsugeh clan categorizes the potency of the visions into roughly 5 categories based on four key factors: time, transparency, totality, and teleology. Time simply refers to when the events occur, whether far in the future or nearly immediate. Transparency is how easily one can glean useful information from the vision. Some visions manifest as vague poems, while in others you witness events with all your senses. Totality is the amount of information one gains from a vision. Teleology is the ability to control the time, totality, and subject matter of the vision. 

Category one visions lack depth in all key factors. They are purposeless, nearly immediate, insubstantial, and incoherent. Often, these visions are no more useful than inductive reasoning for the purpose of predicting the future. Category two visions grant clearer information, but remain purposeless, insubstantial, and generally immediate. Situationally, these visions can be useful, but relying on them to predict something useful is akin to gambling. Category three visions give clear information at an extended timeframe, but lack substance and teleology. Any seer who receives category three visions and above are well regarded by Tsugeh society. Category four visions possess depth in time, transparency, and totality, but lack teleology. Category five visions possess all four key factors. 

Most Augurs are forcibly recruited by the Tsugeh clan as soon as their ability manifests. To the Tsugeh, being an Augur is the highest calling, a sacred duty that often requires one to live a life apart from the world to better interpret the will of their goddess.

## Symbols and Manifestations

**Symbols:**

- A perfectly woven, unending tapestry.
    
- An unbroken golden circle or an ouroboros biting its own tail.
    
- A single, flawless crystal lens or a calm, all-seeing eye.
    
- A perfectly balanced scale.

**Manifestations**

Ilytania rarely manifests in human form, instead appearing as golden wisps, guiding mortals to their destined path.
