True Neutral

Aridia is the Divine personification of the brown dwarf at the center of the star system. Quite literally outshined by her more radiant sibling, it is rarely seen from Panomar. It is only visible during a stellar eclipse, where she interposes herself in the path of Fecundus' light. 

It is the patron of those invisible to society. Beggars, orphans, unrecognized talents, the impoverished, and the lonely see identify with it. Its eclipse is commemorated with community feasts and gatherings. During the commemoration, it is traditional to reveal a personal secret, such as a crush on an associate, or regrets from times past. 

This is the patron deity of the Vagabond Union.
