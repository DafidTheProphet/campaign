## What is Cultivation?

Cultivation is a process in which one gathers refines the energy of the world to improve their mind, body, and soul. Ordinarily, the energies of the world are not compatible with the human body. The totality of the world is incomprehensible and uncontainable. Its truth is overwhelming to the human mind; its power is uncontainable in the human body. 

Cultivators must use techniques to interpret and refine the world's energy. Techniques embody a singular truth or ethos, and when used, alter the energy of the world to fit its image. The cultivator meditates, channels the technique to gather and refine energy, and integrates it with their body, mind and soul. The altered energy enhances the cultivator and molds them through the technique's ethos. 

Even when altered according to a technique, the human body can only withstand so much energy. To advance in power, cultivators must gather energy in stages. They begin at the body refinement stage, then progress sequentially to the qi gathering, foundation establishment, core formation, nascent soul, and peerless immortal stages. Each stage grants greater power than the previous stage, but the complexity and resources required to advance is even increases faster. 

The most prominent, tried, and tested techniques are known as paths. Cultivators across generations have walked these paths to the nascent soul stage. Upon adopting a path, it becomes the foundation for one's cultivation. One can adopt miscellaneous techniques, whether standalone or from other paths, but they can only advance through the stages with their core path. 


## Cultivation in Society

"With great power, comes great responsibility. The responsibility to do what? Whatever you feel like doing." - Harkhan Cairn

No government can exist without force to impose its laws upon the people. Democracy, for instance, channels the sheer numbers of the masses as an invisible force of change. If those in power disobey the masses, they are removed legally, but if they refuse further, then the masses can riot. A single man cannot stand against the masses; but what would happen if this was not the case? There would be no foundation for democracy. 

The cultivators of this world occupy almost all positions of power. A single cultivator can eliminate an army. Their power commands respect, and respect commands authority. The Diune Senate of Asmea is composed of ten cultivators of core formation stage and above. The Dominion of Sun and Moon and the Kingdom of Lekrith are ruled by monarchs at the nascent soul stage of cultivation. Every major nation's government is comprised of collections of high-level cultivators. The sole exception is the Free Isle of Aenwidia, where those who rule have little to no cultivation. Instead, their command over magic, technology, and the unique geography of their domain allow them to remain independent. 

## Cultivation Alchemy

The energy of the world permeates all things. For various reasons, it can accumulate in some places and be sparse in others. The powerful organizations have already claimed the most concentrated spots of energy. Yet, even with these advantages, it is still not enough to advance beyond foundation establishment through meditation alone. 

Cultivation alchemy concentrates and refines the world energy found in divine plants and spirit beasts into pills. These pills grant a considerable amount of qi (world energy) to the user. Some may have additional effects, positive or negative. Regardless, all factions seek new deposits of qi rich matter to make pills for themselves. 

## Limitations of Cultivation

Many can begin their journey, but only a few can rise to the top. To this date, only one man has ascended to the ranks of peerless immortal. Though many have walked the common paths, nascent soul stage is the final wall that none have overcome. The secret to ascending to immortality cannot be learned through cultivation alone.
