Alenias was born before the throes of war, and his destiny led him to bloodshed. As the son of a prominent elder of the Adamantine Fist sect, he was subjected to harsh training throughout his childhood. From dawn til dusk, he smashed rocks with his fists and bludgeoned training servants to temper his body. His masters' efforts were in vain. No amount of training could prepare Alenias for the conflict about to come. 

The Orthodox-Demonic war began when he was only 14, barely after his onset of puberty. The Dominion of Sun and Moon army had advanced halfway into the Mereth-Mar holding territories, and they rapidly approached Asmea. Out of obligation toward his clan and his guiding ethos, he sought the battleground of men and steel. He fought armies and killed numerable enemy combatants. During a decisive strike against the Scribes of the Deathless Scripture, his squad was ambushed. After killing his comrades, the Scribes raised them as undead. Channeling his rage, Alenias brutalized his comrades' bodies one after the other. By sheer luck, he managed to kill the head Scribe and escape to a nearby mountain range. From that moment forward, he swore to never swing his fist in anger. He entered solitary cultivation and emerged as a disciple of the Guiding Palm. 

Now over 200 years old, he is the Pontiff of the Guiding Palm sect. Along with Grand Elder Riven Calderis, they preside over the Diune Republic of Asmea. Though Alenias fondly regards Riven, their governing philosophies clash, and their supporters follow suit. Continuous disagreements deadlock the senate, much to his delight. Very little gets through this great deliberative body, and what survives is often vetoed soon after.
