Spatre is a wild, continent barely held together by the small remnants of humanity. They live in isolated settlements surrounded by vast swathes of wilderness, fending for survival on a daily basis. Their culture is fractured based on the continent's elemental energies: water, fire, earth and wood. The typical balance of the elements is broken. 

With the absence of the metal element, the wood element runs rampant. The land teems with life energy. The animals evolve into powerful spirit beasts capable of slaying powerful cultivators. Plants contain potent energies vital for cultivation alchemy. For an advanced civilization from Jetztara, it is a ripe land ready for harvest.
