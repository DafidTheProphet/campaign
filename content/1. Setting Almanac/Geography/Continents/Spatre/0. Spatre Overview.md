Shaped like an oblong trapezoid, Spatre is bisected by the Shiar-Mohr strait. Above the strait, closer to the equator, lies a vast savannah interspersed with pockets of rainforest. Below the strait lies tundra flood plains and an archipelago extending from the southwestern shore.

The human populace is fractured into innumerable individual duchies, though there are four overarching cultures: water, fire, earth and wood. The typical balance of the elements is broken due to the missing of a metal clan, which would normally counter the wood clan. Because of this, the land teems with life energy. The animals evolve into powerful spirit beasts capable of slaying powerful cultivators. Humanity lives in isolated settlements surrounded by vast swathes of wilderness, fending for survival on a daily basis.
