# A Treatise on the Mereth-Mar Cataclysm and the Hypothesis of the God-Engine

**Professor Lorian Vess, Senior Fellow of Antiquities, The Great Lyceum of Aenwidia**

## Introduction:

For centuries, the accepted narrative of our continent's genesis has been one of natural, albeit apocalyptic, geological fury. The official position of the Mereth-Mar Sect and the consensus among Jetztaran scholars is that the great volcano Mereth-Mar, in an act of pure natural violence, erupted and thus reshaped our world. This "Eruption of Heaven" is taught as the event that cleared the way for our own civilization's rise from the ashes of a primordial world.

After two decades of independent research, funded by the Lyceum of Aenwidia, I can no longer, in good conscience, support this simplistic and comfortable myth. The evidence, though fragmented, points to a far more terrifying and profound truth: the Eruption of Heaven was not a natural disaster. It was an industrial accident on a cosmic scale, and the Palandrians were not its victims; they were its architects.

## The Geological Inconsistencies:

The primary argument against the "natural volcano" theory lies in the evidence itself.

1. Uniformity of Destruction: A natural volcanic eruption, even one of immense power, is chaotic. It leaves zones of greater and lesser destruction. Yet, geological surveys show that the initial blast wave leveled the continent with an almost uniform, symmetrical force, an impossibility for a standard geological event.

    
2. The Nature of the Ash: Analysis of the deepest ash layers reveals trace elements and isotopic signatures that do not correspond to any known natural mineralogy on this planet. They are the byproducts of immense, focused energy manipulation, the likes of which our own industrial arts cannot yet comprehend.
    
3. The Archipelago: The floating islands of the Archipelago of Mereth-Mar are not mere chunks of rock hurled into the sky. Recent inquiries into the nature of these islands show that their suspension is powered by interspersed mythallaric crystals. The conditions under which these form do not exist in nature. The only known method to synthesize them requires colossal magical power. 
    

## The Hypothesis: The God-Engine of Mereth-Mar

Based on these inconsistencies and fragmented Palandrian texts recovered from lesser ruins, I propose a new theory. The Palandrians, in their hubris, built an enigmatic device within the heart of Mereth-Mar, and whether purposefully or not, caused a magical explosion on an apocalyptic scale. 

It is well known that the Palandrians did not merely inhabit the lands around Mereth-Mar. They carved out a system of caverns and passages within the mountain, few of which survive today. There is evidence that they had entire cities hidden within the mountain. . 

I believe they constructed a massive construct or "engine" deep within the mountain's core. Its purpose is unknown, but based on the scant evidence it is clear that this was no mere power plant. It was meant for a singular purpose, and their flawed execution resulted in unprecedented backlash. This was their God-Engine.

The Eruption of Heaven was the result of its catastrophic failure. When the engine overloaded, it did not simply explode like a bomb; it vented its unimaginable power directly upwards, through the mountain that served as its housing. This explains the uniform blast wave and the unique energy signatures found in the ash and the Archipelago. The Palandrians were not wiped out by a volcano; they were annihilated by the failure of their own creation, erased from existence in an instant by a power they could no longer control.

## The Unexplored Frontier: The Great Well

To date, no significant, intact Palandrian ruins have ever been found on or around Mount Mereth-Mar. This is often used as evidence against my theory. I argue it is the strongest point in its favor. The blast was so absolute, so perfect in its upward trajectory, that it vaporized everything on the mountain's surface.

But what of the engine's foundations? What of the core deep beneath the mountain?

The eruption left behind the Great Well, a mountaintop lake of impossible depth, its bottom never successfully charted. It is my firm belief that the ruins of the God-Engine, the very heart of the Palandrian civilization and the key to understanding their extinction, do not lie on the mountain. They lie at the bottom of it, submerged beneath the waters of the Great Well, waiting in the silent, crushing dark. The exploration of these depths should be the single greatest priority for any who seek the true history of our world.
