Vhiarlat is a country of striking contrasts and vibrant landscapes. Situated at the intersection of a mountainous steppe and a bordering rainforest, Vhiarlat features lush plains and high elevations, offering a diverse range of natural beauty.

The entire nation of Vhiarlat lies within the Raedic Wheel, a large cwm (a steep-walled hollow or basin) formed by large mountain ranges called the Merethian spindles, located to the northeast and south. 

Vhiarlat is the proud domain of the Tsugeh Clan, renowned for its devotion to prophecy, magic, and the mind. The country boasts a number of prestigious universities and observatories. Vhiarlat is home to the largest printing station on [[0. Jetztara Overview|Jetztara]], a hub of information and innovation that supports its role as a center of learning and scholarship.
