Located in the East Bay of Asmea, next to the Northern River, Luremark was once a prominent shipping town. Its once bustling port now only hauls cargo to the isolationist [[content/1. Setting Almanac/Geography/Continents/Jetztara/Violet Mountain Empire/index| Violet Mountain Empire]]. The town's economic struggles have forced local officials to turn to less scrupulous industries for survival.
