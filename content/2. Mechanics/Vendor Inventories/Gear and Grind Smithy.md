### Specialized Gear (Non-Magical)

- **Reinforced Gauntlet (Clockwork Grip Enhancer):** A heavy steel gauntlet with intricate gears. Once per short rest, as a bonus action, the wearer can activate it to gain advantage on their next Strength (Athletics) check made to grapple or to maintain a grip. Cost: 200 gp (Stock: 1, likely a custom piece).
    
- **Targeting Monocle (Simple Optics):** A finely crafted monocle with ground lenses, designed to be affixed to a helmet or worn. Provides advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks made to spot distant objects, but not for searching or noticing hidden things up close. Requires an action to focus. Cost: 150 gp (Stock: 1-2).
    
- **Spring-Loaded Bolt Case:** Attaches to a crossbow. Holds 5 bolts and allows the wielder to load one bolt as a bonus action instead of an action, up to 5 times per long rest (then requires careful resetting). Cost: 120 gp (Stock: 1).
    
- **Airship Maintenance Tool Kit:** A sturdy leather roll containing specialized wrenches, spanners, oilers, and diagnostic tools for airship engines and components. Cost: 100 gp (Stock: 2).
    
- ***Gunsmith's Kit:** Tools for maintaining and repairing rudimentary firearms (cleaning rods, small files, oil, spare flints). Cost: 50 gp (Stock: 2-3).
    
- ***Telescopic Spyglass (Simple):** Collapsible brass spyglass, allows for viewing distant objects with x2-x3 magnification. Cost: 200 gp (Stock: 1).
