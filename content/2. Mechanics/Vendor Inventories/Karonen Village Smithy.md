**Specialties:** Farming tools, horseshoes, basic weapon/armor repair, household metal goods.

**Stock (Limited, may need to commission some items):**

#### Weapons (Standard Quality)

- Dagger: 4 gp (Stock: 2-3)
    
- Handaxe: 10 gp (Stock: 1-2)
    
- Javelin: 1 gp each (Stock: 5-10)
    
- Light Hammer: 4 gp (Stock: 2-4)
    
- Mace: 10 gp (Stock: 0-1, might have one made for the local militia captain)
    
- Sickle: 2 gp (Stock: 3-5, essential farming tool)
    
- Spear: 2 gp (Stock: 2-3)
    
- Quarterstaff: 4 sp (Stock: 3-4, often locally sourced wood, iron-shod by smith)
    
- Sling: 2 sp (Stock: 2-3)
    
- Sling Bullets (20): 8 cp (Stock: 2-3 bags, often self-made by villagers)
    
- Shortbow (Simple): 50 gp (Stock: 0-1, might be able to make one)
    
- Arrows (20, basic hunting): 2 gp (Stock: 1-2 quivers, fletching might be rough)
    
- *Flintlock Pistol (Old, unreliable): 150-200 gp (Stock: 0-1, kept under the counter, a relic or trade-in. Smith might not have powder/shot for it).
    
- *Lead Balls & Gunpowder (if pistol available, 5 shots): 4 gp (Stock: 0-1 set, likely old).
    

#### Armor (Standard Quality)

- Padded Armor: 10 gp (Stock: 1, if any)
    
- Leather Armor (Repaired/Used): 15 gp (Stock: 0-1, taken in trade)
    
- Hide Armor: 20 gp (Stock: 1, made from local game)
    
- Shield (Wooden, metal boss): 15 gp (Stock: 1-3)
    
- Shield (Steel, plain, possibly dented): 20 gp (Stock: 0-1)
    

#### Adventuring Gear & Tools (Focus on Practicality)

- Crowbar: 4 gp (Stock: 1-2)
    
- Hammer: 2 gp (Stock: 3-5)
    
- Sledgehammer: 4 gp (Stock: 1)
    
- Pitons (10): 1 gp (Stock: 2-3 sets)
    
- Manacles (Simple Iron): 4 gp (Stock: 1 set, for the village lock-up)
    
- Chain (10 feet, iron): 8 gp (Stock: 1-2 lengths)
    
- Grappling Hook (Crude but functional): 3 gp (Stock: 0-1)
    
- Spikes, Iron (10): 2 gp (Stock: 2-3 sets)
    
- Hunting Trap: 10 gp (Stock: 2-4)
    
- Smith's Tools (Basic set): 30 gp (Stock: 0-1, if an apprentice is leaving)
    
- **Farming Implements:** (Plowshares, scythe blades, hoe heads) – Various prices, 2-20 gp. This is their main trade.
    
- **Horseshoes:** 1 gp per set of four. (Stock: Plenty)
    
- **Nails, Hinges, Latches:** Various small prices.
    
- ***Simple Gear/Pulley System Parts:** (For well, mill, or barn hoist) - Can fabricate simple gears or repair existing ones. Cost varies.
    

#### Services

- Repair Tool/Weapon/Armor: 15-40% of item's cost, depending on damage and material availability. Simple repairs are common.
    
- Shoe Horse: 2 gp (includes shoes).
    
- Craft Basic Item on Commission: (e.g., a spear, simple shield, more pitons). Price of item + 20-50%. Requires time and often upfront material cost if not readily available.
    
- Sharpening: 5 sp - 2 gp.
    
- *Attempt to Repair Rudimentary Firearm (if one is present): Highly risky, may not have parts. 20-50 gp, no guarantees.
