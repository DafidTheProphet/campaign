## **Iron Juggernaut Poison

_Poison (Injury), Very Rare_

This viscous, silver-black fluid glints with metallic flecks and smells of superheated iron. It is designed to replicate the overwhelming force of a catastrophic impact.

A creature subjected to this poison must make a **DC 17 Constitution saving throw**.

On a failed save, the creature is poisoned for 1 minute. While poisoned in this way, the creature's skeletal structure is wracked with microfractures, making it incredibly fragile and granting it **vulnerability to bludgeoning damage**.

At the start of the poisoned creature's next turn, its body is seized by a cataclysmic spasm that mimics the impact of an unstoppable force. The creature takes **6d6 bludgeoning damage** and **6d6 poison damage** as its own musculature crushes its brittle skeleton, sending shards of bone pulverizing its vital organs. The vulnerability to bludgeoning damage ends at the end of the creature's next turn. 

On a successful save, the creature takes half as much damage (6d6 poison) as its muscles only spasm violently, and it suffers no other effects.
