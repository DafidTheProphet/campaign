Born from the perilous forests of Lekrith, the Evyngar royal family's signature martial art, the Path of the Thousand Poisons, makes full use of the deadly poisons of their homeland. 

# Path Features

**Cultivation Modifier**: Dexterity or Constitution\
**Hit dice**: 2d8 per cultivation stage \
**Hit points**: 2d8 (or 9) + Constitution modifier

## Reforged Martial Body: Hundred Poisons Constitution

- You are resistant to poison damage and have advantage on saves vs poison. 
- Your blood acts as a poison. When subjected to this poison, a creature must make a DC 14 CON save or have their movement speed halved and take 2d6 poison damage per turn for 4 turns. The target may attempt a saving throw every subsequent turn. You may cut yourself to coat a weapon in blood as a bonus action (receiving 1d4 slashing damage).

## Path Initiate Technique: Nine Earthly Hidden Weapons

- As an action, you may throw a number of hidden weapons (darts or daggers) equal to your cultivation modifier at targets of your choosing. If you have not taken this action on your turn, you may use your bonus action to throw one dart. 
- You may dip hidden weapons  in poisons you possess as a free action. This includes cutting yourself to use your blood poison, which deals 1d4 slashing damage. The user only receives this damage once per turn, regardless of how many hidden weapons are coated in the blood poison.
- As an action, you may spend 1 qi point to recall any hidden weapons thrown.

## Internal Path Technique: Thousand Poisons Constitution
The poisons in your body has been further refined by intensive cultivation. 

- You subtract your cultivation modifier from any poison damage you receive, and add your cultivation proficiency to constitution saving throws.
- In addition, your blood poison becomes more potent. It now deals 3d6 poison damage per turn and the save DC increases to 16. 
- You may elect to deal acid damage instead of poison damage when using your blood poison. If you choose to do so, the enemy does not suffer a movement speed penalty, and instead receives a -1 penalty to their armor class upon a failed save. 

## External Path Technique: Thirteen Heavenly Daggers

#### Needle Maelstrom

- As an action, spend 4 qi points to throw thirteen daggers in a cloud (5m sphere). This sphere can appear anywhere within 30 ft. Upon contact, an enemy must make a dexterity saving throw or take 10d4 damage and suffer the effects of any poison coating the daggers. You may use a bonus action to move the cloud 15 ft in any direction. This lasts for 3 turns. 

#### Lionfish Mane

- As a reaction, spend 2 qi points to create a shield of hidden weapons. Until the start of your next turn, you have a +3 bonus to AC, including against the triggering attack. If the triggering attack is an unarmed strike, they take 4d4 piercing damage and potentially suffer the effects of any poison coating the hidden weapons. 

## Path Law: Ten Thousand Poisons Constitution

- Your blood poison is even more potent. It now blinds the enemy upon a failed save, and its save DC becomes your cultivation save DC. 
- You are immune to all poisons and poison damage. 
- As an action, you may spend 5 qi points to emit a cloud of your blood poison in a 10 ft radius around you. The cloud of poison moves with you, but it can be dispersed with a strong wind. Any enemy that comes within this cloud must save or suffer the effects of your blood poison. Additionally, this area becomes heavily obscured, though you treat this area as if it is unobscured.
