In the Dominion of Sun and Moon, all who hope to gain power and status practice the path of the Blood Dragon. The practitioners of this path endure the harshest trials, betray their allies, and plunder resources to gain an edge over their opponent. 

# Path Features

**Cultivation Modifier**: Constitution or Charisma \
**Hit dice**: 2d10 per cultivation stage \
**Hit points**: 2d10 (or 11) + Constitution modifier

## Reforged Martial Body: Unhindered Destruction Constitution

You sacrifice your defenses and inner well-being to inflict the maximum amount of damage on your enemies.   
- Until you reach core formation, your lifespan decreases as you cultivate. Upon reaching Qi refinement, your lifespan reduces by 10 years. It reduces by an additional ten years as you advance to Qi condensation and Foundation establishment. You do not gain the lifespan bonuses at these stages. 
- You may pre-emptively strike an attacker as a reaction to prevent their attack.  When an enemy attacks you, you immediately respond with a melee counter-attack (can be a melee cantrip). If your attack lands, the enemy must make their own attack with disadvantage. If your attack doesn’t land, the enemy makes their attack with advantage. You may take this reaction a number of times equal to your cultivation proficiency bonus. 
- Additionally, any temporary hit points you acquire are converted to a necrotic damage bonus added to a weapon attack. This bonus disappears upon landing a successful attack. You may only have a necrotic damage bonus from one source. 

## Path Initiate Technique: Vitality Harvest

- Once per turn, you may spend a qi point as part of a melee attack roll to activate this ability. On a hit, you gain temporary hit points equal to half your damage. On a miss, you lose an additional qi point. Additionally, the target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or suffer from a lingering effect:
	- If the target fails the saving throw, it gains vulnerability to necrotic damage until the end of your next turn, as its life force is destabilized.
- If you have used this ability the turn before, you may not use it on your current turn. 

## Internal Path Technique: Sacrificial Demonic Foundation

- Abyssal Surge: You can expend a hit die to add your hit die roll to the damage of your next weapon attack. This damage is necrotic. You may only use this ability once per turn. 
- When you use vitality harvest, you also regain hit points equal to your cultivation proficiency. 
- Life Drain: Whenever you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with a melee attack, you can use your reaction to regain hit points equal to your cultivation modifier + your proficiency bonus.

## External Path Technique: Blood Ignition

- You roar, igniting the blood in your veins. You spend 3 qi points to slash yourself multiple times (taking 5d6 slashing damage). 
- You may choose to do the following with your blood sphere:
	- As an action, you ignite the sphere and hurl it at an enemy. It explodes upon contact, forcing all within 15ft to make a constitution saving throw. If you are below half health when using this technique
		- Upon failure, the targets suffer the following:
			- They receive 4d8 fire damage and 4d8 necrotic damage 
			- The target is blinded until their next turn
		- On a success, they take half damage and are not blinded. 
		- If you are below half health when using this technique, you may regain 1 qi point if a creature drops to 0 hp. 
	- As a bonus action, you coat your weapons in your burning blood. The blood sphere is consumed upon use. 
		- For 5 turns, your melee attacks deal an extra 1d6 necrotic and 1d6 fire damage
			- On a hit, you may choose to end this effect early, dealing an extra 1d6 necrotic damage and 1d6 fire damage per remaining turn. 
		- When you reduce a creature to 0 hp, you gain temporary hit points equal to your cultivation modifier + your cultivation proficiency.

## Path Law: Ego Consumption

- You are willing to sacrifice everything to defeat your foe. You may spend 5 qi points to immediately drop to 1 hp. 
	- Your next melee attack that hits before the end of your next turn deals bonus necrotic damage equal to the HP lost, up to 50 damage.
	- If this attack hits, the target is also stunned until the end of their next turn.
	- If the attack misses, the effect is lost, and the qi is spent.
- Once per turn, when you hit with an attack or technique that deals fire, necrotic, or blood-related damage, you may declare draconic dominion:
	- The target must succeed on a Charisma saving throw vs your Cultivation DC or become subjugated for 1 round:
		- While subjugated the creature is cursed: they cannot cast spells, activate techniques, or use reactions.
	- A creature already suffering from a blood effect (e.g., bleeding, poisoned by blood-based venom) automatically fails. If the creature automatically failed the previous turn, they do not automatically fail on the current turn.
