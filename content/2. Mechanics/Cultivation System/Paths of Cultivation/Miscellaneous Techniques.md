## Introduction

After gaining a greater understanding of cultivation, players can learn additional techniques outside their chosen path. These techniques come from a variety of places, such as an ancient manual, inscriptions on a stone wall, or a benevolent tutor. Techniques from other paths can be learned as well. 

Players can only learn techniques of a lower level (i.e. foundation establishment cultivators cannot learn core formation techniques). As these techniques differ from your chosen path, you may only learn a limited number of these techniques, equal to half your cultivation modifier rounded down.
