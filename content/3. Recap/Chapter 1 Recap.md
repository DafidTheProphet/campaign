## Part 1: Beginnings

The adventure begins at an inn situated within the Kingdom of Lekrith. Threeknot Barcus, a middle ranked member of the vagabond union, gives the players a task to investigate the village of Naviej. The village of Naviej is a quiet resort town known for their royal mulberries. Recently, the amount of tourists who have gone missing has increased significantly. The players set forth to get to the bottom of this puzzling case. 

## Part 2: The Train

The party boards a train to Naviej, posing as common tourists. On the train, they meet several colorful characters. Fenthic Rowle, a Tsugeh wizard trainee, is beside himself with mania. Chiang Fei, a boisterous disciple of the Adamantine Fist sect, accosts the party during their meal. He is promptly thrown off the train, though not before humiliating Orko.
